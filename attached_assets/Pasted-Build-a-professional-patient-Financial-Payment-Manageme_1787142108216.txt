Build a professional patient Financial & Payment Management module.

IMPORTANT:
- Preserve all existing functionality.
- Do not rebuild or remove the existing Patients, Odontogram, Tooth Clinical Record, Treatment Plan, or Visits modules.
- Do not add a backend.
- Keep the application offline-first.
- Do not use fake patient or financial data.

The Financial module must be the SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH for all financial records.

1. PATIENT FINANCIAL SUMMARY

Create a Financial section inside each Patient Profile showing:

- Total treatment cost
- Total paid
- Remaining balance
- Credit balance if payments exceed the current treatment cost
- Number of payments
- Payment status:
  - Unpaid
  - Partially Paid
  - Paid in Full
  - Credit

Calculate all values automatically.

2. TREATMENT COSTS

Allow Treatment Plan items to have:

- Treatment ID
- Patient ID
- Tooth ID(s)
- Procedure
- Description
- Estimated cost
- Final cost
- Currency
- Status
- Date

Do not duplicate treatment items.

The Financial module must reference existing Treatment Plan IDs.

3. PAYMENTS

Allow the clinician to record multiple payments for the same patient.

Each payment must contain:

- Payment ID
- Patient ID
- Date
- Amount
- Currency
- Payment method
- Visit ID (optional)
- Treatment Plan ID (optional)
- Notes
- CreatedAt
- UpdatedAt
- DeletedAt

Payment methods:

- Cash
- Card
- Bank Transfer
- Electronic Wallet
- Other

4. PARTIAL PAYMENTS

A patient may:

- Pay the full amount at once.
- Pay a deposit before treatment.
- Pay in multiple installments.
- Pay different amounts during different visits.

The system must automatically calculate:

Total Treatment Cost
-
Total Payments
=
Remaining Balance

5. PAYMENT ALLOCATION

Allow a payment to be:

- General patient payment
- Linked to a specific Treatment Plan item
- Linked to a specific visit

Do not create duplicate transactions.

If a payment is allocated to a treatment, the treatment should show the amount paid and remaining amount.

6. FINANCIAL TRANSACTION HISTORY

Create a chronological transaction history containing:

- Date
- Transaction type
- Treatment
- Payment
- Amount
- Currency
- Balance after transaction
- Notes

Make the history easy to read on iPad.

7. MULTIPLE CURRENCIES

Support configurable currencies.

Initially support:

- EUR
- USD
- TRY
- SYP

The default currency must be configurable in Settings.

IMPORTANT:
Do not automatically convert currencies unless an explicit exchange rate is provided.

8. TREATMENT ESTIMATE

Allow the clinician to generate a treatment cost estimate from existing Treatment Plan items.

Show:

- Procedure
- Tooth
- Quantity
- Unit cost
- Total cost
- Overall treatment estimate

Do not create a payment automatically when an estimate is created.

9. RECEIPTS

Prepare the architecture for generating a printable/shareable payment receipt.

A receipt should eventually contain:

- Clinic information
- Patient name
- Patient number
- Date
- Payment amount
- Currency
- Payment method
- Related treatment
- Remaining balance

Do not require a backend.

10. FINANCIAL DASHBOARD

Inside the patient profile show:

- Total
- Paid
- Remaining

Use a clear visual representation.

11. CLINIC FINANCIAL DASHBOARD

Prepare a separate Financial Dashboard for the clinic showing:

- Today's payments
- This week's payments
- This month's payments
- Outstanding patient balances
- Number of patients with unpaid balances

Do not calculate profit yet.
Do not implement expenses yet.

12. DATA INTEGRITY

Financial records must never be permanently deleted.

Use soft deletion:

deletedAt

When a payment is edited, preserve:

createdAt
updatedAt

Do not modify historical transactions silently.

13. DATA ARCHITECTURE

Create clean TypeScript interfaces/types for:

- FinancialAccount
- TreatmentCost
- Payment
- PaymentAllocation
- FinancialTransaction

Use IDs and relationships.

Do not duplicate patient, visit, treatment, or tooth data.

Prepare everything for IndexedDB/Dexie.

14. UX

Optimize for:

- iPad
- Android tablets
- Touch interaction
- Arabic
- English

Use large buttons and clear financial summaries.

Make the interface suitable for real dental clinic use.

15. IMPORTANT SAFETY AND PRIVACY RULE

Do not store card numbers, CVV codes, passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Only store the payment method and transaction information needed for the clinic's records.

Do not introduce a backend.
Do not use mock data.
Do not break existing functionality.
